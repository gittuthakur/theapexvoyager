# Phase 15 — internal supplier and rate library

## Architecture and audit decision

Baseline: `4baa4dcb3381238606b823a579aae7a8d5fd416b` (Phase 14). The worktree was dirty with unrelated HBX, booking, analytics and layout work, all excluded from Phase 15 staging. Audit covered the JourneyCosting domain/calculator/local store, strict internal API validation and development guard, worksheet calculator, TP2 TransportPartner/TransportRate/pricing service, Hotel/provider mapping/rate caches, Journey, Destination and Region models and public serialization paths.

TP2 is an existing supplier transport ledger for exact fixed-route/circuit quotes; Hotel/provider caches serve a different availability workflow. Neither is a general hotel/meal/activity/local-service quote library. This phase extends the existing local costing architecture with reusable supplier/rate domain records and validation. It does not migrate or change TP2, public transport rates, HBX, Google Places, PlaceCache or IndexNow. The Phase 14 exact-supplier transport adapter remains available. There is no production-safe admin authentication; no new production MongoDB supplier collection, fake seed or weak login is introduced.

Combined UI: `/internal/suppliers`; API: `/api/internal/supplier-library`. Existing `/internal/journey-costing` gains explicit supplier-rate selection and quote-request text. Suppliers/rates/revisions live under ignored `.local-journey-costing/supplier-library/`, alongside Phase 14 costing storage. No supplier information is embedded in Journey documents, public serializers or schema.org data. Supplier contacts are absent from financial snapshots and quote helper output.

## Supplier model and verification

`models/SupplierLibrary.ts` defines SupplierInput, Supplier, SupplierRateInput, SupplierRate, RateSelection and RateSnapshot. Supplier fields include name/type/status, optional contact person/phone/email/business name, regions, destinations and private notes. Types: HOTEL, TRANSPORT, ACTIVITY, LOCAL_SERVICE, MULTI_SERVICE, OTHER. Status: ACTIVE, INACTIVE, PENDING_VERIFICATION. Tax IDs/GST are not required. No credential/payment fields exist.

Data classification is explicitly REAL or DEMO_SYNTHETIC; demo-labelled names must use DEMO_SYNTHETIC. The coverage dashboard excludes these suppliers and their rates and excludes demo costings, so local QA cannot fabricate real business readiness. New suppliers are pending/unverified by default, not silently verified. ACTIVE is an explicit operator choice; it is separate from verification.

UNVERIFIED, CONTACTED and VERIFIED are independent metadata. VERIFIED requires explicit `VERIFY <supplier-id>` acknowledgement and verification notes. Verification timestamps are server-derived. Every edit/verification creates a revision; changing identity/contact/area/notes clears prior verification. Status-only deactivation preserves verification history but stops new costing selections. Contact fields are validated, displayed only on internal forms, not logged or passed to analytics/communications helpers. Historical snapshots retain their original identity after deactivation or later edits.

## Rate model and entry

Rates reference an existing non-inactive supplier and have category/subcategory, exact destination, optional location/region, INR currency, required validity dates, season label, ESTIMATE/QUOTED/CONFIRMED, quote reference, private notes and supplier tax inclusion. Categories: HOTEL_ROOM, TRANSPORT_PER_KM, TRANSPORT_PER_DAY, TRANSPORT_FIXED_ROUTE, MEAL, ACTIVITY, PERMIT, LOCAL_VEHICLE, LOCAL_SERVICE, MISC. Category determines a strictly validated hotel, transport or service payload; incompatible/unknown fields are rejected.

Hotel: property, room category, configurable meal-plan abbreviation plus human meaning and explicit included meal labels; single/double/triple rates, extra-adult and child-with/without-bed rates, guest basis, mandatory/weekend/peak supplements and minimum stay. Double rate is required; optional unquoted amounts stay blank, not invented. Room rate is per room-night. Supplements are per room-night; extra adult/child amounts per guest-night. Selected weekend/peak/child supplements require quoted values, including explicit zero if included. Included meals require human-readable plan meaning; CP/MAP/AP labels alone do not silently imply a meal promise.

Transport: configurable vehicle, PER_KM/PER_DAY/FIXED_ROUTE/SUPPLIER_QUOTE basis, applicable supplier rate, minimum km/day, explicit dead-km policy and separate rate where needed, driver allowance/day, night allowance/count, toll/parking/state-tax/permit inclusion flags, pickup/drop and optional exact Journey slugs for a whole-circuit quote. Per-km quantity is max(entered km, days × minimum km/day); dead km and allowances are explicit supplements. PER_DAY quantity must equal selected days. Unpriced excluded toll/parking/tax/permit costs warn and need separately quoted manual lines if package-included. No public ₹13/₹16/₹25 cab rates are imported. Local union/snow/shuttle vehicles remain independent, excluded by default.

Service rates support configurable name, PER_PERSON/PER_GROUP/PER_ROOM/PER_DAY/FIXED and an entered rate. `includedByDefault` is fixed false; operators choose inclusion in the costing. Activities are not hard-coded products. Tax inclusion describes a supplier quote and does not select a business GST treatment.

Rate creation and rate revisions do not promote quote confirmation. A changed quote must be explicitly marked ESTIMATE, QUOTED or CONFIRMED by the operator; owner approval never converts it automatically.

## Validity, seasons and revision history

Date-only ISO validity is inclusive. EXPIRED = validTo before today's IST date; FUTURE = validFrom after today; EXPIRING_SOON = an otherwise current rate with at most 30 calendar days remaining; ACTIVE otherwise. The 30-day threshold is an internal display rule, not an expiry extension. Expired rates remain visible/history-filterable but cannot populate a new costing. Future rates may be explicitly chosen for covered future travel dates. Quote-window boundaries, category and active supplier are checked server-side.

Distinct seasonal contracts are separate records. “Edit / create revision” preserves the same identity and appends a version. Supplier/category cannot change on an existing rate identity; create a new record instead. UUID, version, parent version, creation/update timestamps and immutable JSON files preserve history. Latest-version preconditions reject stale writes. A shared exclusive local write lock serializes library changes. Inspect a stale lock after a crash locally before removing it. Local filesystem access is the trust boundary and requires private backups; Windows privacy follows local account permissions rather than assuming POSIX mode is sufficient.

## Costing integration and snapshots

Manual entry stays available. “Add from Supplier Rate” uses exact category/destination/supplier/date filters, with no cheapest or fuzzy automatic selection. Choose a specific revision, covered service date range, quantity/nights/km/days, occupancy and explicit supplement counts. Preview the financial effect before adding. Hotel/transport defaults may be base costs; activity/local service default excluded and require explicit inclusion.

The server resolves the latest rate and creates a deep snapshot: supplier ID/name/data classification, rate ID/revision, full relevant quoted rate, validity, confirmation and selection basis. It excludes supplier contact records. Financial fields are deterministic, read-only in the UI and validated against the snapshot in the calculation service. Saving resolves the referenced historical revision and checks its exact contents; forged financial/provenance fields are rejected. Later master changes do not dynamically refresh existing costing. Re-select when scenario room/traveller counts change. “Convert snapshot to manual rate” explicitly clears provenance and resets confirmation to ESTIMATE; it does not pretend a manually changed amount is the old supplier quote.

Hotel-included meals display instructions to use Phase 14's linked zero-cost INCLUDED_IN_HOTEL basis. A positively charged meal whose explicit meal type (or exact label) matches a snapshot's included meal is rejected if its date overlaps that stay or is unspecified. A separately dated meal outside the stay is allowed. Operators must identify meal types consistently; arbitrary free-text labels cannot be semantically understood by the engine. Supplements do not inherit implicit traveller/room multipliers. Financial formulas for markup, target margin, commission, tax, contingency and rounding remain Phase 14's existing pure engine.

## Confirmation and owner workflow

Phase 14's stricter rule is retained: READY_FOR_OWNER_REVIEW and OWNER_APPROVED require every included cost to be CONFIRMED, complete and unexpired, plus season/validity. QUOTED can be selected/saved/compared but cannot pass this gate. Overall rollup never calls ESTIMATE or QUOTED supplier-confirmed. Excluded unresolved items remain visible. Owner review/approval records local acknowledgement, not authenticated owner identity.

Journey publication and commercial changes remain separate. The Phase 14 apply action stays PREVIEW_ONLY; no executor or Journey write dependency is added. No existing Journey price/status/itinerary/slug is mutated. This phase does not execute publication tooling or save demo supplier/rate data to production.

## Coverage dashboard

Coverage is calculated from all actual draft Journey context and latest REAL supplier rates. It reports hotel rate coverage across route destination candidates, explicit whole-route transport rate mappings, complete confirmed saved costings, awaiting quotes/costing and expired REAL rates. Hotel coverage is conservative: destinationSlugs may include excursions rather than overnight stops. Every card says the overnight plan must be confirmed by the owner and shows missing candidate quotes, duration, read-only itinerary-derived transport day titles and local-service terms actually mentioned. Unknown km/leg scope remains unknown. A partial local leg never counts as whole-circuit coverage merely because its destination matches.

Fully costable is not inferred from matched master rates alone: it requires a non-demo saved costing with confirmed rollup, positive supplier subtotal, hotel and transport costs and no engine review blockers. Other service/inclusion completeness remains an owner review responsibility, explicitly stated by the dashboard. No invented suppliers or prices exist. Initial verified business counts: hotel 0/12, transport 0/12, complete confirmed costing 0/12, awaiting quote/costing 12, expired REAL rates 0. Demo QA does not change these counts.

## First real supplier and quote workflow

1. Use the existing private dev setup; for a new server bind explicitly: `npm run dev -- --hostname 127.0.0.1 --port 3100`. Open `/internal/suppliers`. Keep the server local; do not tunnel or proxy it publicly.
2. Add the first real hotel supplier with REAL classification, actual name, HOTEL type, factual contact details and service locations. Default PENDING_VERIFICATION/UNVERIFIED is correct until actual checks. Mark CONTACTED if contacted; record VERIFIED only after actual checks with acknowledgement/notes. Choose ACTIVE explicitly when it is eligible for costing selection.
3. New rate → select that supplier → HOTEL_ROOM. Enter the actual property, destination, room category, meal-plan meaning and included meals, double quote and any real optional occupancy/supplement values, tax inclusion, quote reference, season and actual validity. Mark QUOTED when received; CONFIRMED only when actually confirmed. Save. A later revised quote gets another immutable revision; another season gets a separate rate.
4. Add the first real transport supplier (REAL, TRANSPORT) similarly. Select the correct transport category/basis and actual quoted rate, vehicle, validity, minimum km, allowances, dead-km policy and inclusion flags. For an actual whole-Journey circuit quote, enter the exact Journey slug in Journey slugs; do not map a partial leg to the entire tour. Save QUOTED, then confirm only when appropriate.
5. Open Journey costing; select a draft such as Auli Tour and enter occupancy/season/validity. In the picker choose hotel category, exact destination, supplier, rate, covered hotel stay dates and actual nights/occupancy/supplements. Preview and add snapshot. Link included meals at zero cost. Repeat for the quoted transport route with actual km/days/allowances; add separately quoted exclusions and other real services. Save DRAFT. No Journey writes occur.
6. Use the quote helper to generate hotel/transport request text for missing inputs. It includes dates, occupancy, meal-plan/tax/supplement questions and itinerary/vehicle/km/allowance/exclusion questions. Unknown values are marked to confirm; hotel nights at one property are not guessed from total tour nights. Copy text manually. The helper sends no email/WhatsApp and creates no booking.
7. Obtain real confirmations, resolve expiry/missing inputs and use the existing strict owner-review workflow. Keep publication and price execution outside Phase 15 authorization.

## Security and verification

Server page/API guards reuse Phase 14's production-disabled, loopback and same-origin protections. Production returns 404 before rendering props, DB/store reads or request-body mutation. API responses are no-store/noindex. POST requires exact Origin; bodies are bounded; nested unknown fields, malformed IDs/revisions, nonfinite/negative values, invalid enums/dates/currency/contact data and verification injection are rejected. There are no public `/suppliers` or `/vendors` routes. No contact logging, analytics calls, public directory or communications automation was added.

Fixtures and the browser harness are DEMO / SYNTHETIC and local/test-only. QA creates hotel/transport suppliers, multiple seasonal rates, immutable revisions, selected snapshots and later rate changes; saved costing stays unchanged. Focused tests include production denial, privacy, explicit choice, provenance, formula regression, meal duplication, validity and local history. Desktop/mobile controls are exercised at 1440/390/320 with no overflow. An isolated release build uses committed baseline plus Phase 15 files to exclude unrelated worktree changes, then production-mode page/API denial is checked. Read-only production verification compares full Journey fingerprints and public 40/28/12, catalogue/sitemap and draft isolation.

Phase 15 is committed locally only. Push/deployment awaits separate explicit owner chat authorization. Verification evidence: `docs/phase-15-supplier-rate-library-verification.json`; local screenshots/raw audit output: ignored `.tmp/phase15-qa/`.
